# Code of conduct

This is a small project and it should stay a pleasant one to work on. The
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
is the standard this project follows; the short version:

## Expected

- Be direct about code and kind about people. "This is wrong because X" is
  welcome and useful. "You are wrong" is not.
- Assume the other person is competent and working with different information.
  Ask what they know that you do not.
- Critique the design, not the designer. Technical disagreement is the point of
  the project; there is no obligation to agree.
- Assume nothing about who is on the other end — experience, language, time zone,
  or how much time they have.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory comments or imagery, including about race, gender, sexuality,
  disability, or religion.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or dismissing contributions rather than
  addressing them.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Advocating any of the above as a joke, since it reads the same either way.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers through GitHub — open an issue that says only
that you need to speak to a maintainer privately, or use the contact method on the
[repository owner's profile](https://github.com/hibbault). Reports are handled
privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, or block accounts.
They will explain a decision unless doing so would expose the reporter, and they
are held to this document too.

---

This is a deliberately short policy rather than a verbatim copy of the
Contributor Covenant, so that it can be maintained. Where it and the Covenant
disagree about intent, the Covenant is the reference.
